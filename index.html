☕ The Coffee–Fried Chicken Index 🍗

Every London hex scored from −1 (all fried chicken) to +1 (all coffee) — and what that says about house prices

The index

🍗 −10+1 ☕

score = (coffee − chicken) / (coffee + chicken), smoothed over neighbouring hexes. Grey gaps = fewer than two shops nearby.

Layers

Index × house prices

Cafés are a leading indicator of gentrification — a new coffee shop predicts rising local house prices (Glaeser, Kim & Luca 2018), while takeaway density tracks deprivation (Maguire et al. 2015). Correlation, not causation — and hexes are areas, not people.
