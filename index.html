Cafés track affluence and takeaway density tracks deprivation (Glaeser, Kim & Luca 2018; Maguire et al. 2015) — which is what the level correlation shows. The growth result is the 2010s London story: prime districts stalled while Dagenham, Leyton and Abbey Wood caught up from a low base. Correlation, not causation — and hexes are areas, not people.
